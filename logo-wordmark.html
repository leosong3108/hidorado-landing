Dorado · wordmark explorations

Three directions with the wordmark as the hero — no symbolic mascot. Each variant includes a "solo mark" (16–44px) for favicons and UI corners where the full wordmark doesn't fit. Pick the one that clicks, I'll polish and ship it to landing + product + favicons.
